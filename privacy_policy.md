To provide our core functionalities, our App requests the following permissions on your device:

*   **Location Data (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`):** 
    We require your location data to show maps, routes, and provide location-based services within the App. This data may be used while the app is in use to provide accurate geographical features.
*   **Audio Recording (`RECORD_AUDIO`, `MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS`):**
    We require microphone access to allow you to record audio natively within the App for its intended features. 
*   **Camera and Photos (`expo-image-picker`):**
    We request access to your device's photo library and camera to allow you to upload, select, and share photos with your friends or other users within the App.
